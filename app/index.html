    Event log

      What is real here. The six chips are the same integer models that were compared bit by bit with the Verilog designs. The connections between the organs (heart rate to kidney pressure, the glucose balance fed by meals, insulin and glucagon) and the healthy ranges are simple rules of this app; they are not validated physiology. The detector combines the flags raised by the chips, values outside the healthy range of this model, and four patterns across organs. “Connect a chip” takes the reports of a real chip from a serial port or a Bluetooth serial module in place of the simulated one; no chip has been fabricated yet, so this path is untested. Research model, not a medical device.
